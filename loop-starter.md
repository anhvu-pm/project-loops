# My morning project desk

## Set up the desk

Choose the areas you use to organize your work. Under each one, list your projects and the sources you already check, such as a Teams thread, meeting notes or a decision log. Pick a workspace page or file where you can keep the latest dashboard and your decisions between runs.

## Give your assistant this brief

```text
Prepare my morning project dashboard.
My areas, projects and source locations: [list]
Previous dashboard and decisions: [location]
Morning review time and time zone: [time and zone]

Use the latest accessible evidence for each project. Show the time of the review.
Compare the last 24 hours with the previous state. On the first run, use dated
evidence for changes and mark anything that needs a baseline.

At the top, show a few highlights that affect my plans today. Then give me one
ranked list of actions across all projects. For each action, name the project,
the decision or follow-up, and why it deserves that position today. Link to the
project section. As a starting rule, weigh deadlines, people blocked and the
consequence of waiting. Flag unclear priorities for my judgment.

Below the overview, group projects by area. For each project show:
- Current progress toward its next milestone.
- What changed in the last 24 hours, with a dated source for each change.
- Next key actions in priority order, with owner and due date when known.

Carry forward unresolved decisions. If a source is unavailable, say which one.
If the sources were checked and nothing changed, say so. Keep my recorded
decisions when refreshing the page.
```

## Make tomorrow’s review work

Run the brief once and check the first ranked action against its source. In a scheduler that can read your project sources and write to your chosen page, set the brief to run every morning before your review, in your time zone. Check that the next scheduled run updates the same page and displays its new review time. Until a scheduled run succeeds, you can run the brief manually.

After you act, add a dated line to the project section with your decision, what you’re waiting for and when to check again. Tomorrow’s review can then tell you whether that follow-up arrived or still needs your attention.
